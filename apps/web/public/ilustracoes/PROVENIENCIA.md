# Proveniência das ilustrações

Ilustrações das telas de acesso (`/entrar`, `/entrar/2fa`), geradas por IA com a geração de
imagem do ChatGPT (OpenAI) pelo mantenedor, em 2026-11-03, a partir dos prompts abaixo. Os
termos da OpenAI cedem o resultado a quem gerou; o projeto as distribui sob a mesma licença do
repositório (AGPL-3.0). Não retratam um animal real.

Originais: PNG 1672×941. Aqui ficam só as versões otimizadas (AVIF e WebP em larguras menores).
Para trocar uma ilustração, mantenha o mesmo nome e as mesmas larguras e atualize este arquivo.

## caramelo-varanda (computador)

```
Painterly digital illustration in the warm storybook style of the Zustand docs bear art:
soft visible brush strokes, rich warm light, gentle depth of field, lush but calm.
Subject: a Brazilian mixed-breed "caramelo" dog (vira-lata caramelo) — short caramel coat,
white chest patch, semi-erect floppy ears, kind eyes — sitting relaxed on the doorstep of a
simple Brazilian house at late afternoon, as if just arriving home. Potted plants, a bit of
painted wall and tiled floor, golden sunlight from the upper right.
Composition (critical): the dog sits in the right half of the frame, head around 60% from the
left and 40% from the top. The LEFT 40% of the image, especially the lower-left area, must be
calm and low-detail (soft out-of-focus wall/floor), because a white sign-in panel will cover it.
The top-left corner must also be calm and slightly darker, for a white logo and headline.
Palette: caramel, warm browns, muted greens, soft terracotta, warm cream light; no saturated
orange-yellow (reserved for the UI). No text, no letters, no logos, no paw-print motifs,
no frames, no watermark. Not childish, not 3D render, not photo.
```
